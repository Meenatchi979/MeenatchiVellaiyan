About

The story behind the code

I'm currently pursuing my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at CMS College of Engineering, Tiruchirappalli — a decision I made because I wanted to understand not just how software works, but how it learns.

My foundation is in Python and SQL, built on a solid understanding of OOP, DBMS, and data structures. From there, I've been deliberately widening my lens — computer vision, NLP, and machine learning workshops — because I believe a good AI engineer needs to see the whole pipeline, not just one layer of it.

I care about writing code that's easy to read six months later, and I'd rather ask one extra question than ship one wrong assumption. That's the quiet discipline behind every project I take on.
